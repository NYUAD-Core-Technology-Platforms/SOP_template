# KIT-MEG System — Step-by-Step Operating Procedure

## Pre-Experiment Setup (Prior to Participant Arrival)

### Prepare the Lab Equipment
1. Check the KIT-MEG system's operation status
2. Ensure the Helium level is sufficient
3. Gather empty room data and check noise levels

### Configure Systems

**On the Stimulus2 Computer:**
1. Wake the Vpixx
2. Run the test script
3. Ensure the Vpixx screen image in the MSR room displays correctly

**On the MEG Main PC:**
1. Turn on the MEG Main PC and open the MEGLAB program
2. Turn on the DAQ PCs by selecting **Acquire → Wake-Up DAQ**

**Other Systems:**
1. Turn on the camera screen behind the Stim PC
2. Turn on the communication soundboard (flip switches to ON)
3. Wake HeadScan computer system in preparation room

### Room Preparation
1. Fresh linen, clear tape, and earbuds should be set up inside the MSR
2. Set up camera monitors, and ensure any signs for female participants are in place
3. For participant safety, call security to request they switch off their radios

### Other Preparations
- Phones should be in airplane mode
- The heater element in the MSR should be turned off

---

## Experiment Procedure (Following Participant Arrival)

### Informed Consent
1. Obtain the participant's informed consent and demographic information
2. Confirm no metal objects are on the participant's body
3. Change participant into scrubs

### Participant Setup
1. Seat the participant in a static chair for head scanning
2. Mark the participant's face for laser point placement (1-7) using the HeadScan system
3. Scan the participant's head and register laser points 1-7

### Positioning the Participant in MSR
1. Enter the MSR room with the participant — **ensure both of you remove your footwear**
2. Power on the marker box and place 5 markers on the participant's face
3. Lay the participant down with a comfort pad under the knees, positioning the head inside the KIT-MEG system
4. Clean and insert earbuds into the participant's ears
5. Place the left or right button boxes (Vpixx or Legacy) in the participant's corresponding hand

### Final Setup Inside MSR
1. Confirm the correct placement of all markers and earbuds

> **IMPORTANT:** Close and lock the MSR door before starting the procedure.

---

## MEG Data Acquisition

### System Tuning and Preparation
1. On the MEG Main PC, navigate to **Acquire → Autotuning** to adjust for environmental noise
2. Open the recording window by selecting **Acquire → MEG Measurement**
3. Lock the sensors to ensure accurate data capture

### Signal Quality Check
1. Perform a marker measurement
2. If the result is above **90%**, proceed with the experiment

### Start Recording
1. On the MEG Main PC, begin recording MEG data by selecting **Start** in the Measurement pane
2. On the Stimulus2 Computer, navigate to **Experiments** and configure the stimulus material
3. Monitor the participant on the video screen and check in periodically via the communication system

---

## Post-Experiment Procedure

### End of Experiment
1. Abort the recording by pressing **Abort** in the MEG Measurement window
2. Perform one last marker measurement to complete the session
3. Unlock the sensors before opening the MSR door
4. Open the door to the MSR and assist the participant in exiting the system

### Cleanup and Shutdown
1. Remove the head position coils and dispose of earbud foam
2. Exit the MSR with the participant and complete any ERB protocol requirements
3. Transfer the recorded files from the MEG Main PC
4. Shut down the DAQ PCs by selecting **Acquire → Shutdown DAQ → Shut Down Data Acquisition Equipment**
5. Turn the heater element back on by pressing the **POWER** button

## Maintenance & Service

No maintenance tasks are required from the end user. The system is under service contract and all maintenance is performed by the manufacturer's representatives. The responsible person ensures that the manufacturer's representative performs repairs and maintenance in a safe fashion.

### Routine Cleaning & Maintenance by User

**Computer Systems:**
- Do not update any software on the DAQ, Control, or Stim PCs without prior approval from the responsible person
- Do not install any programs on the DAQ, Control, or Stim PCs without prior approval
- Do not connect USB or other external storage devices to the DAQ, Control, or Stim PCs without prior approval

**Other Equipment and Accessories:**
- Ensure that the laboratory space is kept tidy
- Any components or accessories that come in contact with human participants must be disposed of, or cleaned and sanitized before re-use

### Decontamination/Sterilization

**Dispose of after contact with participants:**
- Tissues
- Disposable ear pieces/headphones
- Tape

**Wipe with 70% isopropyl alcohol:**
- Head position indicator coils
- Response boxes

**Wash in laundry machine:**
- Bedding and blankets kept within the MSR
- Hospital scrubs worn by research participants

Place dirty linen in the special container (dirty linen).
