# QD Helium Liquefier — Helium Refilling Procedure

## Pre-Refilling: Record Data in Logbook

Before the helium refilling, record the following data in the logbook:

1. Amount of liquid helium in the MEG in %
2. Gas counter reading
3. Medium Pressure (MP) Hub screen: outlet P
4. ATP screen: volume of purified gas, pressure (P) input, P output
5. ATL screen: L volume

---

## Helium Transfer Procedure

### Prepare the ATL

1. Stop ATL by pressing **MODE → STOP → OK**
2. Disconnect gas inlet, supply, return and electrical connector from the rear side of ATL
3. Release wheel brake if necessary
4. Switch the yellow valve handle to **TRANSFER** position (horizontally)
5. Move ATL to MEG room

### Connect the Transfer Line

1. Press green **START** button on the MEG level-meter
2. **Put on protective face shields and adequate cryo-gloves**
3. Release and remove the plug from the transfer port
4. Carefully insert the transfer line into the port

> **IMPORTANT:** Do not bend the transfer line! It must remain vertical at all times.

5. Stop inserting when a thick portion of the transfer line passes through the sealing washer — tighten the washer
6. Slowly lower the transfer line until white plume comes from the other side
7. Insert this side in the MEG inlet port; seal the port by tightening the washer if necessary
8. Slowly lower the transfer line into ATL until it touches the bottom, then pull it 3-5 cm back

### Start Refilling

1. Start refill by pressing **MODE → TRANSFER → 5**
2. Press **CANCEL** if compressor activation is requested
3. Touch the screen again to return to the main menu
4. Observe helium level change in ATL (decreasing) and MEG (increasing)

### Terminate Refilling

Refill should be terminated when **one or more** of the following conditions are fulfilled:

- ATL helium level below **10L**
- MEG helium level above **90%**
- Red overfill light on the wall is activated

### Disconnect

1. Lift the transfer line by **60-70 cm**
2. Wait approximately **30 seconds**
3. Unplug the line from the MEG port
4. Quickly remove the transfer line from ATL
5. Press the red **STOP** button on the MEG level-meter
6. Press green **POWER** button on the wall box on MEG

---

## Post-Refilling: Restore ATL

1. Move ATL back into the soundproof enclosure
2. Activate the wheel brake
3. Switch the yellow valve handle to **BPC** position (vertically)
4. Connect the gas inlet, supply, return and electrical connector from the rear side of ATL
5. Start ATL by pressing **MODE → LIQUEFY → SLOW**

---

## Post-Refilling: Record Data in Logbook

Record the following data in the logbook:

1. Amount of liquid helium in the MEG in %
2. Gas counter reading
3. Medium Pressure (MP) Hub screen: outlet P
4. ATP screen: volume of purified gas, pressure (P) input, P output
5. ATL screen: L volume

**Procedure is complete.**

---

## ATP Regeneration Check

> **IMPORTANT:** If the difference between P input and P output on ATP screen is **4 PSI or higher**, please make sure the Medium Pressure (MP) Hub outlet pressure is below **5 bar** and start ATP regeneration by pressing **MODE → REGENERATION → LOW TEMPERATURE REGENERATION → OK**.
